# REQUIEM OF KINGS
### Project Vision & Creative Direction

> **Core Philosophy:**  
> Chess is the strategy.  
> Fantasy is the world.  
> Combat is the spectacle.  
> The two must work beautifully together.

---

## 1. Creative Identity & Mission

**REQUIEM OF KINGS** is a cinematic, dark-fantasy 3D chess experience designed to merge authentic chess rules with the atmosphere, visual depth, and spectacle of high-end fantasy role-playing games.

The project is built by a father for and with his 14-year-old son, celebrating a shared love of authentic chess, Dungeons & Dragons, dark fantasy, mythical creatures, and immersive games.

It is strictly a serious, sophisticated fantasy strategy game suitable for teenagers and adults—never an educational children's game, never cartoonish, and never simplified.

---

## 2. The World: The Ashen Throne

The primary battleground is **The Ashen Throne**, set in a vast, ancient Gothic cathedral throne room once belonging to a mighty dynasty, now contested by supernatural armies:

- **Monumental Gothic Architecture:** Towering ribbed vaulted ceilings, pointed lancet arches, massive weathered stone pillars, and weathered statues flanking the throne dais.
- **Stained Glass & Volumetric Light:** Dramatic stained glass rose windows casting ruby, gold, and sapphire shafts of light cutting through atmospheric cathedral haze.
- **The Obsidian & Pale Stone Board:** A monumental chessboard crafted from mirror-sheen obsidian and pale weathered ancient stone, banded in antique gold and blackened steel with etched runic borders.
- **Atmospheric Environmental FX:** Four monumental iron braziers burning with live flames, rising wisps of smoke, drifting dust and ash motes, and distant thunderstorm lightning illuminating the stained glass.

## 3. The Two Armies

### The Ember Court (Faction One)
An ancient royal dynasty that has survived centuries of war through discipline, blackened steel, and ancient fire magic.
- **Visual Identity:** Blackened forged steel armour, crimson cloaks, burnished antique gold trim, heavy ceremonial weaponry, burning blades, and molten fire magic.
- **Characters:**
  - **King:** *The Ashen King* — Weathered crown, monumental ceremonial plate, legendary executioner's sword.
  - **Queen:** *The Flame Sorceress* — Powerful battle mage channeling devastating pillars of fire and molten energy.
  - **Bishop:** *The Fire Priest* — Mystical cleric wielding a staff topped with sacred flame.
  - **Knight:** *The Ember Paladin* — Heavily armored champion delivering thunderous charging sword strikes.
  - **Rook:** *The Iron Sentinel* — Living fortress of enchanted iron and granite with ground-shattering hammer strikes.
  - **Pawn:** *The Oathbound Soldier* — Disciplined royal infantry with steel shield, sword, and battle-tested plate.

### The Hollow Legion (Faction Two)
A forgotten civilisation raised from the crypts through forbidden necromancy.
- **Visual Identity:** Ancient obsidian armour, carved bone and skeletal detailing, tattered ceremonial fabrics, violet supernatural fire, ghostly illumination, and cursed silver weaponry.
- **Characters:**
  - **King:** *The Hollow Sovereign* — Undead monarch crowned in shattered obsidian and ancient runic plate.
  - **Queen:** *The Deathweaver* — Formidable necromancer summoning spectral chains and violent violet void magic.
  - **Bishop:** *The Plague Oracle* — Supernatural priest channeling cursed dark energy.
  - **Knight:** *The Dread Rider* — Corrupted spectral knight cutting through swirling fog and ghostly flames.
  - **Rook:** *The Bone Colossus* — Towering guardian of fossilized bone and necromantic power.
  - **Pawn:** *The Risen Warrior* — Undead vanguard soldier with weathered armor and cursed blades.

## 4. Gameplay & Combat Non-Negotiables

1. **Chess is Law:** All gameplay is 100% faithful to the official rules of chess via `chess.js` (legal moves, castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate, draw rules). Animations never introduce randomness or alter chess outcomes.
2. **Cinematic Combat:** Captures trigger dramatic cinematic battle sequences with dedicated camera framing, anticipation, weapon swings or ranged spells, particle bursts, screen shake, hitstop, and magical dissolve effects.
3. **Player Agency:** Players can customize combat pacing (Full, Short, Skip) and toggle accessibility options (Reduced Motion, Reduced Flashing, Screen Shake intensity).
4. **Local & AI Modes:** Seamless local two-player hotseat and computer opponent modes with zero required accounts, logins, or online dependencies.
